PlainWalk Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

PlainWalk (the “App”) is designed with your privacy as the first priority.

Information We Collect

The App does not collect or transmit any personal information.

  • Location: Used to draw the roads you have walked onto your blank map. Your location data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to anyone, including the developer.
  • Motion data (step count): The device's motion sensor is used to display your step count for today. This data is also processed only on your device.

Where Your Data Is Stored

All records (trails, pioneered distance, step counts, and so on) are stored only on your iPhone. There is no server, and no account registration is required.

Sharing With Third Parties

Because the App collects no data, there is no information to share with third parties. The App contains no advertising SDKs and no analytics SDKs.

Deleting Your Data

You can delete all records at any time from “Profile > Reset data” inside the App. Deleting the App also removes all of its data from your device.

Permissions

  • Location (While Using the App): Required to record your trail. Recording continues while the screen is off, but stops as soon as you end the recording.
  • Motion & Fitness: Used to display your step count.

Both permissions can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings.
